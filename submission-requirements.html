Submitting to Brighton Rocks or Hastings Rocks?

These requirements apply once your film has been selected for a collection. Submitting your film to the festival itself happens directly through Brighton Rocks or Hastings Rocks.

Brighton Rocks Hastings Rocks

Film Master

If your film is selected for inclusion in a festival collection, we'll need a clean export to master from. We work with whatever you can realistically supply — just flag your format when you send it over.

I.

Resolution & Codec

Full HD 1080p preferred. ProRes, DNxHD or a high-bitrate H.264 export all work — avoid re-compressing an already compressed file if you can help it.

II.

Aspect Ratio & Frame Rate

Whatever your film was finished in — 16:9, 2.39:1, 4:3 — is fine. Keep the original frame rate rather than converting it.

III.

Audio

Stereo or 5.1, at the mix you finished with. Please make sure dialogue levels are consistent from scene to scene.

IV.

Subtitles (optional)

If your film has burned-in or separate subtitle files, let us know — we can author them onto the disc as a selectable track.

What it becomes

This is roughly what your film master turns into once it's authored, packaged and printed — menus, disc art and all.

Artwork & Credits

02
I.

A Still or Poster

One strong image from the film, or a poster if you have one — used for the disc menu and case artwork.

II.

Credits List

Director, producer and any cast or crew you'd like credited on the disc menu and case.

III.

Synopsis

A short synopsis, around 50–100 words, for the case back and menu.

IV.

Runtime & Content Notes

Exact runtime, plus anything we should flag for viewers — flashing imagery, strong language, that sort of thing.

“A separate agreement is always required before your film is reproduced or sold — registering interest or submitting to a festival never signs that away.”

Rights & Permissions

You keep ownership and copyright of your film throughout — nothing here changes that. If your film uses licensed music, archive footage, or other third-party material, please make sure you hold (or can clear) the rights to include it on a physical release before it's selected.

If your film is chosen for a collection, we'll send a straightforward licensing agreement to sign before anything is produced — covering exactly what we're allowed to do with your film, and for how long.
