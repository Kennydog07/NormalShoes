Who we are

Normal Shoes began with a simple observation: graduating filmmakers pour a year or more into a final project, and then it quietly disappears into a folder of exports and forgotten drives. We wanted to give that work a proper, physical conclusion.

We work with individual filmmakers, cohorts and film departments, mastering finished work to Blu-ray and handling the design and print that goes around it — treated with the same care a released film would get.
