# Granny & Stevie: Character and Style Guide
*Series by Ida-Mae Mann*

Use this guide with every illustration so Granny, Stevie and the house look the same on every page and in every book.

## Granny
- Straight blonde hair, shoulder length, with a side parting
- Big teal-blue eyes and a warm, slightly mischievous smile
- Usual outfit: black sleeveless top
- Always wears a thin gold bangle on one wrist
- Personality: playful, patient and easily delighted, and she cries happy tears at big moments

## Stevie
- Baby, about 9–15 months old across the series
- Bright ginger-orange hair with one big curl sticking up on top (the signature curl)
- Big teal-blue eyes (the same colour as Granny's), round rosy cheeks
- Usual outfit: pale pink top or vest
- Personality: curious, determined, giggly and a little cheeky

## Granny's house
- Cosy living room with a big **blue sofa**
- **Orange cushion with a cream diamond pattern**
- A **table lamp with an orange shade** that gives off a warm glow
- A **green plant in a terracotta pot** on the windowsill, by cream curtains
- Lighting: warm, golden and evening-soft

## Art style
3D animated feature-film look: soft rounded shapes, big expressive eyes, warm lighting, gentle colour palette. Use the same style as the original *Granny & Stevie* cover.

**Reusable image prompt (paste it in front of each page's illustration note):**
> 3D animated children's film style, warm cosy lighting. Granny: shoulder-length straight blonde hair, big teal eyes, black sleeveless top, thin gold bangle. Stevie: baby with bright ginger hair and one big curl on top, big teal eyes, rosy cheeks, pale pink top. Granny's living room: blue sofa, orange diamond-pattern cushion, orange table lamp, potted plant on windowsill.

## Writing style used in these books
- Gentle rhyming couplets, 2–4 lines per page, easy to read aloud
- 12 story pages per book, plus a final "heart" page
- Every book ends with the series refrain:
  **"Granny loves Stevie, and Stevie loves Granny."**
- Stevie is always called "Stevie", never "he" or "she", so the books work whichever way you picture Stevie

## Suggested reading order
1. Stevie Learns to Walk
2. Stevie Says "Granny!"
3. Splish-Splash Bath Time
4. Granny and Stevie Go to the Park
5. Stevie Won't Go to Sleep!
