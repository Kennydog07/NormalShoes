# Granny and Stevie Go to the Park
*A Granny & Stevie book, by Ida-Mae Mann*

**Cover:** Granny pushes Stevie on a baby swing. The ginger curl is blown back by the wind, and autumn leaves swirl around them.

---

**Page 1**
"Coats on!" said Granny. "Shoes on! Hats too!
We're off to the park, just me and just you."

*Illustration: Granny wrestles a tiny shoe onto Stevie's foot by the front door. Stevie is already pointing outside.*

---

**Page 2**
Down the path Stevie toddled, holding Granny's hand,
with wobbly new walking, just like they'd planned.

*Illustration: Granny and Stevie walk hand in hand from behind. Granny takes tiny steps to match Stevie's.*

---

**Page 3**
At the pond there were ducks: one, two, three!
"QUACK!" shouted Stevie. "DUCK! Just like me!"

*Illustration: Three ducks on the pond. Stevie flaps both arms like wings. (A nod to the bath duck from book 3.)*

---

**Page 4**
Granny tossed seeds, and the ducks gobbled fast.
One cheeky duck chased poor Granny right past!

*Illustration: A bold duck waddles after Granny. Granny tiptoes away with exaggerated alarm, and Stevie is in fits of giggles.*

---

**Page 5**
Next came the swings. Up, up, up high!
"Wheee!" squealed Stevie, almost touching the sky.

*Illustration: Stevie mid-swing, mouth wide open in delight. Granny pushes from behind.*

---

**Page 6**
"Higher?" asked Granny. "Just a little bit more?"
"MORE!" shouted Stevie. A brand-new word to explore!

*Illustration: Stevie points up to the sky, saying "MORE!" in big letters. Granny looks surprised and proud.*

---

**Page 7**
In the sandpit they dug and they patted and piled,
made a big sandy castle. Granny smiled and smiled.

*Illustration: A lopsided sandcastle with a leaf flag. Stevie has sand on the nose.*

---

**Page 8**
Then Stevie sat down, right on top with a THUMP.
The castle went SQUISH. Now it's more of a lump!

*Illustration: Stevie sits happily on the flattened castle. Granny has her hands on her cheeks in mock horror.*

---

**Page 9**
Crunchy leaves, crunchy leaves, orange and brown.
Stevie kicked them up, and they all floated down.

*Illustration: Autumn leaves fly. Some are the same orange as Stevie's hair, and one lands on the curl.*

---

**Page 10**
Granny made a big pile and said, "Ready... set..."
Then she jumped in herself! The best jump yet!

*Illustration: Granny mid-leap into a leaf pile with her blonde hair flying. Stevie claps.*

---

**Page 11**
The sky turned pink, and the air turned cool.
"Time to go home now, my little jewel."

*Illustration: A sunset over the park. Granny zips Stevie's coat. Stevie yawns.*

---

**Page 12**
Stevie rode home up on Granny's back,
fast asleep before they'd reached the end of the track.

*Illustration: Granny carries a sleeping Stevie piggyback. A leaf is still stuck on the ginger curl.*

---

**The heart page**
Ducks, swings and sandcastles, leaves in the air.
The park is the best when Granny is there.

**Granny loves Stevie, and Stevie loves Granny.**

*Illustration: Back home on the blue sofa, both asleep under a blanket, lamp glowing.*
