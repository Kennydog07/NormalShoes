# Stevie Says "Granny!"
*A Granny & Stevie book, by Ida-Mae Mann*

**Cover:** Stevie, mouth open mid-word, points up at Granny. Granny has a hand on her heart and her eyes are shining.

---

**Page 1**
Stevie could babble. Stevie could coo.
Stevie could blow a raspberry or two. *PBBBT!*

*Illustration: Stevie blows a big raspberry. Granny is pretending to be splattered.*

---

**Page 2**
"Can you say *Granny*?" Granny asked with a grin.
Stevie just giggled and grabbed Granny's chin.

*Illustration: Granny leans close. Stevie squishes her cheeks with both chubby hands.*

---

**Page 3**
At breakfast Granny pointed at herself. "Gran-ny! Try!"
"NANA!" shouted Stevie, as proud as a pie...

*Illustration: Stevie in a high chair, very proud, with Granny pointing at her own chest.*

---

**Page 4**
...and pointed straight at the fruit bowl, you see.
"Banana!" laughed Granny. "Well done! But not me!"

*Illustration: Stevie's finger points at a bunch of bananas. Granny laughs with her head thrown back.*

---

**Page 5**
Out in the garden: "Say *Gran-ny*, my dear."
"BIRD!" shouted Stevie. A robin was near.

*Illustration: Granny and Stevie on a garden bench. A cheeky robin is perched on the fence.*

---

**Page 6**
At bath time Granny tried once again.
"DUCK!" splashed Stevie. Granny laughed, "Not yet, then!"

*Illustration: Stevie in the bath holding up a yellow rubber duck. Granny is wet from the splash.*

---

**Page 7**
At lunch Stevie's spoon went flying. Oh no!
"UH-OH!" said Stevie, as it fell down below.

*Illustration: A spoon of orange mush mid-air. Granny dives to catch it.*

---

**Page 8**
"Banana, bird, duck and uh-oh," Granny said.
"Four words already, you clever little head!"

*Illustration: Granny counts on her fingers. Stevie holds up four fingers (or tries to).*

---

**Page 9**
That night, as the lamp made the living room glow,
Granny sang softly, so sweet and so slow.

*Illustration: The cover scene. Stevie is sleepy in Granny's arms on the blue sofa in the warm lamplight.*

---

**Page 10**
She tucked Stevie in, then she turned to go.
But a little voice whispered, so soft and so low...

*Illustration: Granny tiptoes towards the door. Stevie is peeking over the cot rail.*

---

**Page 11**
# "GRAN-NY!"

*Illustration: Large, joyful lettering across the page. Stevie stands in the cot, arms reaching out.*

---

**Page 12**
Granny stopped. Granny turned. Granny's eyes filled with tears.
The best word she'd heard in all of her years.

*Illustration: Granny scoops Stevie up. A happy tear runs down her cheek and her bangle glints.*

---

**The heart page**
Bananas and birdies and ducks are just grand,
but one little word is the best in the land...

**"Granny!"**

**Granny loves Stevie, and Stevie loves Granny.**

*Illustration: Nose-to-nose cuddle, with both sets of teal eyes sparkling.*
