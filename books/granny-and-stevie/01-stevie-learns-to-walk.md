# Stevie Learns to Walk
*A Granny & Stevie book, by Ida-Mae Mann*

**Cover:** Stevie stands by the blue sofa, holding on with one hand and wobbling, with the curl bouncing. Granny kneels a few steps away with her arms wide open.

---

**Page 1**
One sunny morning at Granny's house,
Stevie was crawling, as quick as a mouse.

*Illustration: Stevie crawls fast across the rug. Granny laughs from the sofa holding a cup of tea.*

---

**Page 2**
Crawl to the cushion, crawl under the chair,
crawl round the kitchen. Stevie crawled everywhere!

*Illustration: A playful montage of Stevie peeking out from under a chair, beside the orange cushion and in the kitchen doorway.*

---

**Page 3**
But Granny walked tall on two feet, not four.
"Hmm," thought Stevie. "So what are feet for?"

*Illustration: Stevie sits on the rug looking up at Granny's feet as she walks past, with a puzzled frown and the curl tilted.*

---

**Page 4**
Stevie grabbed the sofa and pulled up high.
Wibble went the knees. "Oh my, oh my!"

*Illustration: Stevie stands holding the blue sofa with knees wobbling and eyes wide. Granny's hands are clasped in delight.*

---

**Page 5**
BUMP! Down went Stevie with a soft little plop.
"Good try!" Granny cheered. "Don't you dare stop!"

*Illustration: Stevie sits down on a nappy-padded bottom, surprised rather than upset. Granny is clapping.*

---

**Page 6**
Up again, Stevie! Hold on tight.
One hand, two hands, that's just right.

*Illustration: Stevie is standing again with a determined face and tongue poking out in concentration.*

---

**Page 7**
Granny knelt down at the edge of the rug.
"Come here to Granny for a great big hug!"

*Illustration: Granny kneels a few steps away with her arms wide open. Her gold bangle catches the light.*

---

**Page 8**
Stevie let go. The whole room went quiet.
Should Stevie stay put? Or should Stevie try it?

*Illustration: Stevie stands alone with arms out for balance. Even the plant on the windowsill seems to lean in to watch.*

---

**Page 9**
One step... two steps... wibble and wobble...
three steps... four steps... a teeny bit topple!

*Illustration: A sequence of Stevie's little steps across the rug, with dotted footprints showing the path.*

---

**Page 10**
Five steps, and WHOOSH! Into Granny's arms!
Stevie had WALKED! Ring the bells! Sound the alarms!

*Illustration: Stevie lands in Granny's arms. Both are beaming, and little stars and sparkles burst around them.*

---

**Page 11**
Granny danced Stevie all round the room,
held up high like a bright orange balloon.

*Illustration: Granny twirls Stevie overhead. The ginger curl flies, and the lamp glows warmly behind them.*

---

**Page 12**
Now Stevie walks to the window, walks to the door,
walks to the kitchen, and back for some more!

*Illustration: Stevie walks proudly and a little bow-legged towards the camera. Granny follows with her arms ready, just in case.*

---

**The heart page**
And at the end of every wobbly walk,
Granny is waiting... *(and Stevie's learning to talk!)*

**Granny loves Stevie, and Stevie loves Granny.**

*Illustration: The two of them cuddled on the blue sofa in lamplight, just like the original cover.*
