# Splish-Splash Bath Time
*A Granny & Stevie book, by Ida-Mae Mann*

**Cover:** Stevie sits in a bubble-filled bath with a bubble "hat" on top of the ginger curl. Granny is beside the tub, soaked and laughing.

---

**Page 1**
"Bath time!" called Granny. "Let's run it nice and warm."
Stevie heard the water and crawled in like a storm!

*Illustration: Stevie zooms down the hallway towards the bathroom. Granny is checking the water with her elbow.*

---

**Page 2**
In went the bubbles, in went Ducky too,
in went Stevie. "Splash time for you!"

*Illustration: A yellow rubber duck bobs among the bubbles. Stevie is lowered gently into the bath.*

---

**Page 3**
SPLISH! went a hand. SPLASH! went a toe.
Granny got soaked from her head to her... OH!

*Illustration: Water flies everywhere. Granny's blonde hair drips and she has a "really?" face.*

---

**Page 4**
"Oh, Stevie!" laughed Granny, all dripping and damp.
"I'm wetter than you are, my little bath champ!"

*Illustration: Granny wrings out a strand of her hair. Stevie claps with glee.*

---

**Page 5**
Granny made a bubble beard, all fluffy and white.
"Ho ho ho!" she rumbled. Stevie squealed with delight.

*Illustration: Granny sports a huge foam beard. Stevie is giggling helplessly.*

---

**Page 6**
Then Granny made Stevie a tall bubble crown,
balanced on the curl so it wouldn't fall down.

*Illustration: A bubble crown sits on Stevie's head with the ginger curl poking through the top.*

---

**Page 7**
Ducky went diving. Ducky went *quack*.
Ducky swam off, and then ducky swam back.

*Illustration: Stevie watches the duck swim away, eyes following it closely.*

---

**Page 8**
Now hair wash time. Oh, Stevie's not keen!
"Look up at the ceiling, we'll get you all clean."

*Illustration: Stevie's grumpy face as Granny gently tips a little cup of water. Granny holds a hand over Stevie's eyes.*

---

**Page 9**
"All done!" sang Granny. "No soap in your eyes!"
Then out came the plug, and a gurgling surprise...

*Illustration: Stevie stares at the swirling plughole in wonder.*

---

**Page 10**
GLUG-GLUG-GLUG went the water. "Bye-bye!" Stevie waved.
"Bye-bye to the bubbles!" Granny said, quite amazed.

*Illustration: Stevie waves at the plughole, and the last bubbles disappear in a spiral.*

---

**Page 11**
Wrapped in a towel, as snug as a bug,
Stevie got dried with a rub and a hug.

*Illustration: Stevie in a hooded towel (perhaps with little ears), with only the curl and teal eyes peeking out.*

---

**Page 12**
Soft pink pyjamas and sweet-smelling hair.
Stevie's all clean, and Granny's damp everywhere!

*Illustration: Stevie fresh and fluffy in pink pyjamas. Granny's top is still clearly soaked and she laughs about it.*

---

**The heart page**
Wet or dry, splashy or neat,
bath time with Granny is always a treat.

**Granny loves Stevie, and Stevie loves Granny.**

*Illustration: Both on the blue sofa wrapped in one big towel, lamp glowing.*
