# Stevie Won't Go to Sleep!
*A Granny & Stevie book, by Ida-Mae Mann*

**Cover:** Stevie is wide awake and grinning on the blue sofa. Granny is next to Stevie, yawning hugely with droopy eyes.

---

**Page 1**
The moon was up, and the stars were too.
"Bedtime!" said Granny. "Night-night, Stevie-Boo."

*Illustration: A night sky through the window beside the potted plant. Granny gestures towards the hall.*

---

**Page 2**
But Stevie's eyes were wide, as wide as could be.
"NO!" said Stevie. "No sleep! Play with me!"

*Illustration: Stevie bouncing on the blue sofa with eyes huge and teal and the curl bouncing.*

---

**Page 3**
So Granny sang a lullaby, soft as a sigh.
Stevie joined in with a "LA LA!" so high.

*Illustration: Granny singing gently. Stevie sings loudly with mouth wide open, and music notes go everywhere.*

---

**Page 4**
Granny read a story, a long one, and slow.
Stevie turned the pages, too fast to know!

*Illustration: Stevie flips the pages of a picture book in a blur. Granny has lost her place.*

---

**Page 5**
Granny gave Stevie warm milk in a cup.
Stevie drank every drop... and then sat right back up.

*Illustration: Stevie with a milk moustache, sitting bolt upright and still wide awake.*

---

**Page 6**
Granny rocked Stevie, to and fro, to and fro.
Stevie's eyes stayed open. Granny's started to go...

*Illustration: Granny in a rocking chair with her own eyelids drooping. Stevie is wide awake in her arms.*

---

**Page 7**
"Let's count the stars," Granny yawned. "One... two..."
"THREE!" shouted Stevie. "Four, five, BOO!"

*Illustration: Both at the window. Stevie points at the stars, and Granny's head is resting on the glass.*

---

**Page 8**
Granny's eyes closed. Her chin dropped to her chest.
Out came a snore. Granny was having a rest!

*Illustration: Granny asleep sitting up on the blue sofa, mouth slightly open. Little "zzz"s float up.*

---

**Page 9**
Stevie looked up. "Granny?" Not a peep.
Oh dear, oh dear! GRANNY's gone to sleep!

*Illustration: Stevie peers up at Granny's sleeping face, surprised and curious.*

---

**Page 10**
So Stevie climbed up onto Granny's lap,
patted her cheek, and gave a soft tap.

*Illustration: Stevie gently pats Granny's cheek. The gold bangle is visible on Granny's relaxed hand.*

---

**Page 11**
Stevie pulled the blanket over Granny's knees,
then snuggled right in and said, "Shhh... Granny... please."

*Illustration: Stevie tucks a blanket clumsily over both of them and holds a finger to the lips.*

---

**Page 12**
Stevie's eyes fluttered, heavy and deep.
And then, at last, Stevie fell fast asleep.

*Illustration: Both asleep, cuddled together on the blue sofa. The lamp casts its warm orange glow.*

---

**The heart page**
Who put who to bed? Well, nobody can say.
But they'll both wake up ready for another big day.

**Granny loves Stevie, and Stevie loves Granny.**

*Illustration: A recreation of the original cover pose, but with both of them asleep. A perfect series ending.*
