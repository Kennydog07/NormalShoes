Make Your Own Discs

The Disc Lab

Your discs are burnt by you, or by a person you nominate who isn't connected to Normal Shoes and isn't paid to burn. We supply everything else: the mastered disc file, artwork, printed blank discs, packaging and help.

What Normal Shoes supplies

We master your film into a disc file, design and print your artwork, and supply printed blank discs (with nothing recorded on them) and packaging. We're on hand to help you through every step.

The one thing we don't do is press burn. The copies of your film are made by you, or by your nominated person.

How It Works

01
I.

Send Your Film

Send us your finished film. We master it into a disc file, with menus and chapters.

II.

We Design & Print

We design your artwork and print it, along with your blank discs and packaging.

III.

Book a Disc Lab

Use the burning equipment at one of our festivals, or book an appointment with us.

IV.

Burn & Pack

You, or your nominated person, burn the discs and pack them, with our help if you want it.

V.

Take Them Home

You leave with your finished set.

Who can burn

You — the filmmaker, burning copies of your own film.

Or someone you nominate — a person you choose, as long as they are not connected to Normal Shoes and aren't paid to burn. Tell us their name when you book.

For festival collections and archives, all the filmmakers in the collection agree one nominated individual who burns the discs for the whole collection. See Submission Requirements.

What to bring

A film you own the rights to, including the music. If your film uses licensed music, archive footage or other third-party material, make sure you hold the rights before you book.

If someone else is burning for you, bring them along, or tell us their name in advance.

Where and what it costs

Burning equipment is available at our festivals, or by appointment.

The Disc Lab is part of each of our package tiers (Gold Tier and Director's Cut), which we quote for your film. For the Hastings Rocks Collection and archive releases, see the prices on those pages.
