Festival Partner

Brighton Rocks International Film Festival

Normal Shoes is proud to be the physical media partner of Brighton Rocks International Film Festival — mastering official selection films onto a lasting Blu-ray collection, volume by volume.

A Physical Record of the Festival

Every year, Brighton Rocks screens 200+ films to a room full of people who came specifically to see them. When the festival ends, most of those films go back to being a private link on a hard drive somewhere. We think the ones that played in that room deserve a more permanent home.

The Brighton Rocks Collection is a multi-volume Blu-ray box set drawn from each year's official selection. Films are grouped into volumes as the collection grows, mastered and packaged the same way as the rest of our work — full HD, proper menus, and a spine that belongs on a shelf next to a director's other favourite discs.

Being included is entirely optional and free to filmmakers who submit — it doesn't replace or interfere with your festival submission, it's a separate keepsake for the films that played.

How It Works

Submit Your Film

Once you're part of the Brighton Rocks official selection, tell us you'd like to be included using the form below.

II.

Grouped Into a Volume

Selected films are grouped by festival edition into a numbered volume of the Brighton Rocks Collection.

III.

Mastered & Authored

We master your film to Blu-ray spec alongside the rest of that volume's line-up — clean menus, no compression shortcuts.

IV.

Yours to Keep

You receive copies of the finished disc — something for your portfolio, distributors, press, or family.
