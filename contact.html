BBFC and the Video Recordings Act 1984

Normal Shoes masters and manufactures discs on behalf of filmmakers, production companies, festivals and educational institutions. Unless a customer chooses our Retail-ready service, discs are supplied on the basis that they are for private, archival, submission or non-commercial use. They are not classified by the BBFC and are clearly marked as such.

Under UK law, video works sold, hired or otherwise supplied to the public on disc must usually be classified by the BBFC and labelled accordingly. If you plan to sell or distribute your film to the public, choose our Retail-ready service, and we will manage BBFC submission and labelling for you. Classification fees are charged at cost.

Every order requires a signed Order Declaration. We may decline or pause any order where the intended use appears to require classification.
