About

The label is normal. The films aren't.

Normal Shoes is a tiny Blu-ray label run from Brighton. We release films nobody else would, and we try to do it properly.

What we release

Three kinds of film, one shelf:

  • Homegrown outsider films. DIY, no-budget, made-with-your-mates cinema that has a following but never got a proper release.
  • Unseen overseas films. Films that have never been released in the UK.
  • Lost or forgotten films. Out of print, never on disc, or surviving on a tape somewhere.

Why it exists

Plenty of films are loved by the people who've seen them and invisible to everyone else. Streaming won't touch them, the big labels can't make the numbers work, and the DVD the director burned in 2008 is getting hard to find.

We think those films deserve a proper release: a good disc, a booklet that tells the story of how they got made, and the people who made them credited and paid. We take them seriously. No “so bad it's good”. If a film is strange, it's strange on purpose, or by accident, and either way it's worth keeping.

Why Brighton

Because it's home, and because it's a town that has always made its own entertainment. Our first release, NS-001, is Made in Brighton. Our first stockist is Timeslip in Brighton. We'd like launch screenings to happen here too. Then we go further afield.

The Made In idea

Shoes come with a little label saying where they were made. So do our films. Every release carries a shoebox-style label with the city, its coordinates, the country and a spine number. Each place gets its own colour, so the shelf slowly builds into a spectrum and the catalogue turns into a map of unseen cinema.

We use cities, not countries: one film can't speak for a whole nation. Where we can, we work with writers, translators and artists from the place the film was made, and we credit them on the box.
