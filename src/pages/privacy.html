3. Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the label, who act on our instructions:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts this website and stores what you submit through our forms, including the mailing list.
  • Stripe processes payments. Your card details are entered into Stripe's secure checkout and handled by Stripe, not us. We receive your name, email, delivery address, what you bought and the last four digits of your card. Stripe also uses payment data to prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google (Gmail) provides our email account. Form submissions, order emails and messages are stored there.
  • Postal and courier services get your name and delivery address when we send your order.
  • Our stockists don't get your details. If you buy in a shop, that's between you and them.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our legal rights.

4. International transfers

Netlify, Stripe and Google may process information in the United States and other countries outside the UK. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK–US “data bridge” where the provider is certified, or the International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum in the provider's data processing terms. Ask us if you'd like more detail.

5. How long we keep it

  • Mailing list and Shoe Club: until you unsubscribe, or after 3 years with no emails sent to you, whichever comes first.
  • Film suggestions and messages: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless they turn into a release or agreement.
  • Orders and sales records: 6 years from the end of the tax year they relate to.
  • Copies held by Netlify: we periodically delete form submissions once they're safely in our own records.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking. It stores one thing on your device: whether you prefer the light or dark theme (in your browser's local storage, never sent to us).

When you open checkout, Stripe loads its payment form and may set cookies that are strictly necessary for secure payment and fraud prevention. If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent first.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to: access the information we hold about you; have it corrected; have it deleted (unless we have to keep it, for example for tax); restrict or object to how we use it; get it in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, email {{email}}. There's no charge, and we'll respond within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

8. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, helpline 0303 123 1113, or Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.

9. Security

The site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we use reputable providers for hosting, payments and email. Only the people at Normal Shoes who need your information can see it, and right now that's one person.
