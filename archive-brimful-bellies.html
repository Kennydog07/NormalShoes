Brimful Bellies & Bad Behaviour

A film by Cal Dalglish.

Synopsis

In this darkly comic short, an ordinary meal becomes an increasingly strange study of appetite, intimacy and control.

Blending uncomfortable humour with striking imagery, Brimful Bellies and Bad Behaviour explores the curious rituals and shifting power dynamics that emerge when hunger — and human behaviour — are allowed to run unchecked.
