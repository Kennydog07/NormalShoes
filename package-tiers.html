Package Tiers

Two ways to bring your film home on physical media — choose the tier that fits your film and your festival run.

Tier One

Gold Tier

A properly authored Blu-ray in a standard case — everything a festival submission or portfolio copy needs.

  • Fully authored Blu-ray disc — menus, chapters, no compression shortcuts
  • Standard Blu-ray case
  • Printed booklet
  • Disc art

Tier Two

Director's Cut

The full physical release — a digipak presentation with extra keepsakes, for festival winners, collectors, or a film you want to do right by.
