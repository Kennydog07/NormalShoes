A Physical Record of the Festival

Every year, Hastings Rocks brings together a room full of people who came specifically to watch the films in its programme. When the festival ends, most of those films go back to being a private link on a hard drive somewhere. We think the ones that played in that room deserve a more permanent home.

The Hastings Rocks Collection is a multi-volume Blu-ray box set drawn from each year's official selection. Films are grouped into volumes as the collection grows, mastered and packaged the same way as the rest of our work — full HD, proper menus, and a spine that belongs on a shelf.

Being included is entirely optional and free to filmmakers who submit — it doesn't replace or interfere with your festival submission, it's a separate keepsake for the films that played.
